Thank you for helping keep our prayer-community safe and secure. We take the security of our platform and the privacy of our members seriously.

## Reporting a Vulnerability

If you discover a security vulnerability within our repository or platform, please **do not disclose it publicly** until our team has had a chance to address it. 

### Where to Report
Please report security issues privately via one of the following methods:
* **Email:** Send details directly to our security maintainers at `security@your-prayer-community-domain.com` *(or replace with your preferred secure contact email)*.
* **Private Advisory:** If hosted on GitHub, you can use the **Report a vulnerability** tab under the Security section of our repository to open a private security advisory.
